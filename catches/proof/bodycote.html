Apollo walked away from Bodycote. We read that as the opening, not the close.

On 8 June Apollo pulled its £1.5bn bid and the market moved on. We did not: a withdrawn offer leaves a board that has already run a process and shareholders who have already seen a price. By 5 August CVC and Veritas had both bid. On 1 September Veritas won the board at 940p, eighty-five days after the bid that failed.
