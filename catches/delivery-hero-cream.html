What we told them
2 June 2026

A cross-border public bid of this size runs for months on competition filings across many jurisdictions, remedy design around the overlapping markets, and integration planning for technology, logistics and shared functions. That work starts at announcement, not at close.

What we said to do
The next move

Approach corporate development at both groups with separation planning and carve-out readiness while regulatory review runs. A 14-market divestment was already pre-agreed, so the carve-out perimeter was knowable before the market had priced it.
