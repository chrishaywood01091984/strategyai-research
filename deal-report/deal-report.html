04 · The sponsors

Capital is recycling,
not sitting still

Private equity was behind sixteen deals directly, but its fingerprints are on far more. The defining sponsor move of the month was the secondary buyout: assets passing from one fund to the next rather than to a strategic or the public market.

Secondary buyouts
Triton bought Flender from Carlyle. CVC bought Irca from Advent. Hellman & Friedman bought Hyve from Providence. Arcline bought Avantus Aerospace from Inflexion. Sponsor-to-sponsor is the exit route of choice.
Take-privates
Ingredion for Tate & Lyle. Mubadala for Pierre & Vacances. Norvestor for Zalaris. A Riesling consortium for Viva Wine. Dai Nippon for AUSTRIACARD. Public markets are a source, not a destination.
Big-ticket sponsors
Bain took a majority of Everllence from Volkswagen at 7.4bn euros. EQT moved on Yorkshire Water's parent. Sixth Street put over a billion into Kpler. Scale capital is chasing platforms, not just add-ons.
Sovereign & global
Masdar into Repsol's renewables. Mubadala into French leisure. Foxconn on Kontron, Dai Nippon on AUSTRIACARD. Gulf and Asian capital is buying European operating assets directly.
◆ Why it matters

A recycling market is a mandate-rich market. Every secondary buyout resets a value-creation plan. Every carve-out needs a standalone operating model. Every take-private opens a hundred-day agenda the day it delists. This is where advisory demand concentrates, and it is forming now, weeks before most of these deals close.

06 · What it means

The takeaways

01 · Bank consolidation is the new headline

Intesa on MPS reopens European FIG consolidation at scale. Watch the Addiko Bank contest (NLB versus Raiffeisen) as the next domino.

02 · It is a carve-out and take-private market

Corporates are simplifying and public markets are a hunting ground. Fourteen carve-outs and seven take-privates in a month is a structural signal, not noise.

03 · Sponsors are trading with each other

Secondary buyouts dominate the exit route. Capital is moving, and every hand-off resets a value-creation clock.

04 · Europe is on sale to global capital

US, Gulf and Asian buyers are acquiring European operating assets directly. Cross-border integration is the mandate that follows.

Methodology & sources

How this was built

Window. The thirty days to 3 July 2026. Scope. Genuine M&A, buyouts, carve-outs, take-privates, mergers, minority and growth investments, and exits across Europe, the Middle East and Africa. Equity and debt capital-markets issuance is tracked separately and excluded here to keep the read on control transactions.

Data. Eighty-three situations curated and verified against public sources by StrategyAI's intelligence engine. Values are disclosed enterprise or equity values in US dollars where public; thirty-nine of the eighty-three carry a disclosed figure, so the 88.8bn dollar total is a floor, not a ceiling. Every deal is source-linked and scored for mandate strength.

What we do not do. We do not blur what is confirmed with what is forming, and we do not publish a company on a single unverified source. Where a figure could not be stood up, it is left out.
