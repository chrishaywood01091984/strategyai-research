What we're seeing

Bank consolidation is the new headline.Intesa Sanpaolo's 30.6bn euro move on Monte dei Paschi reopens European FIG consolidation at scale. Watch the Addiko Bank contest next.
It is a carve-out and take-private market.Fourteen carve-outs and seven take-privates in a month. Corporates are simplifying (Volkswagen, thyssenkrupp, Saint-Gobain, Evonik), and public markets are a hunting ground, not a destination.
Sponsors are trading with each other.The secondary buyout was the exit route of choice: Triton bought Flender from Carlyle, CVC bought Irca from Advent, Hellman & Friedman bought Hyve from Providence. Every hand-off resets a value-creation clock.
Europe is on sale to global capital.US, Gulf and Asian buyers are acquiring European operating assets directly: Ingredion, Energy Fuels, Foxconn's Ennoconn, Dai Nippon, Masdar and Bain all shopping in the window.
